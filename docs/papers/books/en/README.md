# TwinsEnglish Paper Books — English Bound Editions

This directory holds the English bound (collected) editions of the TwinsEarth paper series.

## Volumes

| Book | Scope | PDF |
|---|---|---|
| `en-A_Collected Papers of TwinsEarth` | All 46 numbered papers in one volume | 735 pages |
| `en-C1_Flagship Theory of Intelligence` | P0, P0b, P0c, P0d, P13, P14, P15, P39 | 179 pages |
| `en-C2_Consensus and Security Governance` | P2, P3, P16, P26, P31, P32 | 82 pages |
| `en-C3_Network Coordination and Communication` | P1, P21, P22, P28 | 54 pages |
| `en-C4_Memory, Data and Engineering` | P4, P10, P12, P17, P18, P19 | 89 pages |
| `en-C5_Market, Economy and Evaluation` | P11, P23, P25, P27, P33, P34 | 67 pages |
| `en-C6_World Models, Capability and Industry` | A0, P5–P9, P20, P24, P29, P30, P35–P38, Doubao I & II | 272 pages |

Each volume ships in three formats: `.md` (source text), `.docx` (editable), `.pdf` (typeset, images embedded).

`figures/` holds the referenced figures used by the `.md`/`.docx` sources. PDFs are self-contained.
