## 核心目标

完善 ReputationBridge 和 SettlementBridge 双合约的实现，补充 Foundry 测试。

## 新增内容

### ReputationBridge

- `sendReputationUpdate(destChain, agent, score)`：发送信誉更新
- `receiveReputationUpdate(sourceChain, agent, score)`：接收并验证
- 跨链消息 ID 防重放
- 信誉上下限校验

### SettlementBridge

- `sendSettlement(destChain, taskId, amount, recipient)`：发起结算
- `receiveSettlement(sourceChain, taskId, amount, recipient)`：执行结算
- 分账逻辑：多方收款
- 退款机制：结算失败时自动退款
